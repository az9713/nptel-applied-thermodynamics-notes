References

Official course records and technical sources used in the notes.

  • The original NPTEL catalog identifies Applied Thermodynamics at IIT Madras and Prof. Babu Viswanathan. The expanded catalog credits Prof. V. Raghavan and provides 72 public recordings. The supplied playlist adds one private slot. The source map reconciles catalog and playlist order.
  • MIT Unified Engineering, Thermodynamics and Propulsion develops the balances and air-standard cycle models used to check several derivations.
  • IAPWS-IF97 defines thermodynamic property relations for fluid water in industrial calculations, including saturation. NIST SRD 69 provides independent fluid-property lookups. Report the property reference convention with numerical values for enthalpy and entropy.
  • NASA Glenn’s ideal Brayton cycle shows the relation between the compressor, turbine, and idealized cycle. The isentropic-compression derivation is a check for the calorically perfect gas formulas.
  • NACA Report 1135 gives the quasi-one-dimensional compressible flow equations and normal shock ratios for a calorically perfect gas.
