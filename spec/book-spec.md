# Applied Thermodynamics

This independent study edition follows the lecture order in the supplied NPTEL playlist. The reader knows calculus, mechanics, and algebra and wants to analyze engineering devices from their thermodynamic states, physical boundaries, and measurable flows.

Each chapter starts from a device or decision that motivates the balance equations. A timestamp leads back to the source for each major lecture topic. Derivations state the assumptions that close the model and check a numerical case or limiting case. Exercises give complete solutions. Python laboratories reproduce selected calculations from the notes.

Official course material establishes lecture sequence and attribution. Technical standards and institutional references support added property models and methods. Original examples use clearly identified inputs. Record the coverage of transcripts and visuals independently in the source inventory.
